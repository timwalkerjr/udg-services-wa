- Cards: Soft off-white cards (`bg-[#f7f5f0]` / `border border-[#eae6de] rounded-2xl p-6 sm:p-8`), with glassmorphic dark cards on the hero overlay (`bg-black/30 backdrop-blur-md border border-white/20 rounded-2xl`).
- Badges: Small pill tags (`rounded-full px-3 py-1 text-xs uppercase tracking-wider font-medium`) with orange bullet indicator.
