FAQ

## Quick answers,  _no jargon._

Something not listed here? Send a message and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Do you offer same-day emergency appliance repair?

Yes. For urgent failures -- like a refrigerator or freezer that has stopped cooling -- we offer same-day emergency service across the Tri-Cities. Call us at (509) 300-4042 and we'll dispatch a technician as quickly as possible.

How much does an appliance repair cost?

Every repair starts with a diagnostic visit that covers the technician's assessment. Before any work begins we give you a clear, upfront repair price -- you approve the cost first. No hidden fees, no surprises.

What appliance brands do you service?

We service all major brands including Bosch, GE, LG, Samsung, Whirlpool, KitchenAid, Maytag, Frigidaire, Amana, Electrolux, Kenmore, Jenn-Air, Sub-Zero, Viking and more -- plus older models other companies turn away.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Richland, Pasco, Kennewick, West Richland, Burbank, Riverview and the surrounding Tri-Cities area -- with no rural surcharges or hidden travel fees.

Do you repair both homes and businesses?

Yes. We work with homeowners, landlords and small businesses -- including restaurants, hotels and retail operations that need commercial refrigeration, kitchen equipment and laundry machines repaired fast.
